Why it matters

Your invoice history is also a future-work list

Many independent operators do excellent field work but rely on customers to remember the next pump-out. That leaves revenue to chance. A basic follow-up system helps you contact the right household at a reasonable time, before an urgent problem—or a competitor—makes the decision for them.

You do not need perfect data to begin. Start with completed jobs, record what you know, estimate next-service timing conservatively, and improve the record at every customer conversation.
