Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I download my purchase?

Temvero products are currently sold through Gumroad. After purchasing, use the download link provided by Gumroad or access the purchase using the email address you used at checkout.

I did not receive my download. What should I do?

First, check your inbox and spam folder for your Gumroad receipt. If you still cannot access the file, email support@temvero.com with the product name and the email address used for your purchase.

Can I request a new business template?

Yes. We welcome suggestions for practical tools and templates for small businesses and niche industries. Send your idea to support@temvero.com.
